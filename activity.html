Munnar Tea Trails

Explore the misty hills of Munnar, surrounded by endless tea plantations and beautiful mountain views. Enjoy peaceful walks, cool weather and breathtaking landscapes in one of Kerala's most scenic destinations.

Wayanad Adventure

Discover the wild beauty of Wayanad with lush forests, waterfalls and exciting adventures. Experience trekking, ziplining and stunning views while exploring Kerala's green paradise.

Vagamon Glass Bridge

Walk above the hills and enjoy breathtaking views from Vagamon's famous glass bridge. Surrounded by green valleys and rolling mountains, it is the perfect destination for adventure lovers and nature explorers.
